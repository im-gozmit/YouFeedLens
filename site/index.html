Built for focus

A calmer way to learn online.

YouFeedlens uses the meaning of a recommendation, not a rigid category list, to shape the feed around the subject you care about right now.

✦

Choose any domain

Describe React, economics, system design, or any learning goal in your own words.

◌

Keep the signal

Relevant and closely related videos stay fully visible so useful context is never lost.

◒

Fail open

Uncertain classifications remain visible. You stay in control of what you watch.

Three simple steps

Your feed, your intention.

1

Set a focus

Enter what you want to watch today from the extension popup.

2

Let it sort

Visible recommendation cards are evaluated in small batches.

3

Stay on track

Off-topic recommendations soften into the background as you browse.

Private by default

Designed around your control.

YouFeedlens does not change your YouTube account, subscriptions, recommendation history, or watch history.

What gets evaluated?Only the selected learning domain and bounded metadata visible on recommendation cards: video ID, title, and channel name. No page HTML, comments, search terms, or playback data.
